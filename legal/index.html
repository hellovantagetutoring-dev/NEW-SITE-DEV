Privacy Policy & Terms of Use.

How we handle your information and the terms that apply when you use Vantage Tutoring’s websites (including the Learning Portal) and services in Queensland, Australia.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This page sets out our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for Vantage Tutoring (“we”, “us”, “our”), operating in Brisbane, Queensland, Australia. By using vantagetutoring.com.au, the Learning Portal at learn.vantagetutoring.com.au, or engaging our services, you agree to these terms.

1. Privacy Policy

1.1 Who we are

Vantage Tutoring provides tutoring, masterclasses and related educational support, including an online Learning Portal for enrolled students, parents/guardians using a student’s account, tutors and administrators. For privacy questions, contact us at hello@vantagetutoring.com.au or 0414 131 893.

1.2 What personal information we collect

Depending on how you contact us or use the portal, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number and preferred contact method
  • Whether you are a parent/guardian or student, school, year level and subjects
  • Availability, goals, tutor-tier preference and enquiry details you submit
  • Payment and booking details needed to deliver sessions (for example via invoice or scheduling tools)
  • Learning Portal account details (email, display name, role such as student, tutor or admin, enrolment and tutoring-pair links)
  • Session and calendar information (titles, times, locations/links, reschedule requests and notes)
  • Resource activity needed to protect materials (for example download events and watermarks tying a PDF to an account)
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser type, cookies needed for sign-in, and pages visited (via hosting and standard logs)

We do not ask for more information than we reasonably need to respond to your enquiry, run the portal, or provide tutoring.

1.3 How we collect information

  • Directly from you (enquiry form, email, phone, SMS, Google Meet or in person)
  • From a parent/guardian on behalf of a student under 18
  • When you sign in to the Learning Portal (for example Google sign-in or email one-time link)
  • Through third-party tools we use to operate the business (for example Formspree for form/notifications, Calendly for consultations, Supabase for authentication and database/storage, Vercel for hosting, and Google for OAuth where you choose Continue with Google)

1.4 Why we collect and use information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and match students with tutors
  • Schedule, deliver and improve tutoring and related services
  • Operate the Learning Portal (access control, calendars, resources, reschedule requests)
  • Communicate about sessions, resources, payments and updates
  • Protect our teaching materials from unauthorised sharing
  • Comply with Australian law and protect our legal rights
  • Operate and secure our websites

We will not sell your personal information.

1.5 Children and students under 18

Our services are often arranged by parents or guardians for school-aged students. If you are under 18, please have a parent or guardian submit enquiries and enter into tutoring arrangements on your behalf where required. Parents/guardians may use the Learning Portal with the student’s enrolled email account. We treat student education information carefully and only share it with tutors and staff involved in delivering the service.

1.6 Disclosure to others

We may share personal information with:

  • Our tutors and team members who need it to deliver the service (including seeing calendars, pairs and materials for their students)
  • Service providers who help us operate (for example form processors, email, scheduling, authentication, database/storage, hosting or payment providers), under appropriate confidentiality arrangements
  • Regulators, courts or others where required or authorised by Australian law

Some providers may store or process data outside Australia. Where that occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure information is handled carefully.

1.7 Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Portal resources are access-controlled; downloads may be watermarked. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. You must keep your sign-in method secure and not share portal login links or accounts.

1.8 How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law (for example tax or record-keeping obligations), then delete or de-identify it where practical. Unverified portal sign-ins (emails not on our class or pair lists) may be retained briefly so we can review access attempts and respond to enrolment requests.

1.9 Access, correction and complaints

You may ask to access or correct your personal information, or make a privacy complaint, by emailing hello@vantagetutoring.com.au. We will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

1.10 Cookies and website data

Our sites may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for the site to function (including keeping you signed in on the Learning Portal), or to understand basic usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking essential cookies may stop sign-in from working.
